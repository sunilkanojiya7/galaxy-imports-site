Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Galaxy Imports ("we," "us," or "our") collects from visitors and wholesale customers of galaxyimports.us, and how that information is used.

Information We Collect

  • Business and contact details submitted when applying for a wholesale account (business name, contact name, email address, phone number, shipping and billing address, resale license information).
  • Order history, invoices, and payment records related to your wholesale account.
  • Communication preferences, including any consent you give to receive SMS text message updates.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to process and fulfill wholesale orders, manage your account, respond to support requests, and send you updates about your orders and deliveries, including by text message if you have opted in to receive them.

SMS Communications

If you provide a mobile phone number and opt in to receive SMS updates — either verbally with a Galaxy Imports sales representative or through our website — we will send you text messages regarding the status of your orders and shipments. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP, or request help by replying HELP.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Retention

We retain account and order information for as long as your wholesale account is active, or as needed to provide our services, fulfill legal and tax obligations, and resolve disputes.

Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may withdraw SMS consent at any time, by contacting us using the information below.

Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to Galaxy Imports through galaxyimports.us.
